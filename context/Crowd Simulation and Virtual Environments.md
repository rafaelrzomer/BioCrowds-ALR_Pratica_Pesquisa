## Simulation Models

- Reynolds [11] proposes a model for simulating flocks of birds, herd of animals, and schools of fishes.
- Boids (“bird-oid”) follow three rules:
  - Separation: steer to avoid crowding local flockmates;
  - Alignment: steer towards the average heading of local flockmates;
  - Cohesion: steer to move towards the average position (center of mass) of local flockmates;

![Diagrams illustrating Separation, Alignment, and Cohesion rules.](349ca0a6a9c2e2651a4deeeaf8be6da1_img.jpg)

| Rule Name  | Visual Logic Description                                    | Vector Direction                                                       |
|------------|-------------------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Separation | Central boid (green) surrounded by local flockmates (blue). | Red arrow points away from the nearest flockmates to avoid collision.  |
| Alignment  | Central boid (green) surrounded by local flockmates (blue). | Red arrow points in the average direction of the flockmates' headings. |
| Cohesion   | Central boid (green) surrounded by local flockmates (blue). | Red arrow points toward the center of mass of the local group.         |

Diagrams illustrating Separation, Alignment, and Cohesion rules.
